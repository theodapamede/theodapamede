### Hi, I'm Theo 👋

Physician-scientist and postdoctoral fellow in the [HITI Lab](https://www.hitilab.com/), Department of Radiology & Imaging Sciences, **Emory University**.
I build and evaluate AI for medical imaging, with a focus on **opportunistic screening**: finding hidden signs of disease, such as cardiovascular risk on routine mammograms and chest X-rays.
